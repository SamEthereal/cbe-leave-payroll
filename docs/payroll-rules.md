# Payroll Rules Specification

## Statutory Tax Brackets

### South Sudan (NRA PIT)
- 0 - 2,000 SSP: 0%
- 2,001 - 5,000 SSP: 5%
- 5,001 - 10,000 SSP: 10%
- 10,001 - 15,000 SSP: 15%
- > 15,000 SSP: 20%

### Djibouti (PTS)
- 0 - 30,000 DJF: 0%
- 30,001 - 50,000 DJF: 15%
- 50,001 - 150,000 DJF: 20%
- > 150,000 DJF: 30%

## Overtime Calculations
- Normal Overtime: Hourly Rate $\times$ Hours $\times 1.5$
- Holiday / Weekend Overtime: Hourly Rate $\times$ Hours $\times 2.0$
