# System Requirements Specification

## 1. Functional Scope

### Employee Bio-Data & Profile Management
- Comprehensive 19-section employee master data capture (Personal, Contact, Family, Parents, Dependents, Next of Kin, Education, Qualifications, Languages, Previous Employment, Employment Status, Bank & Statutory details).
- Multi-field search and filter by Job Grade (1-9), Department, Branch, and Employment Status.

### Promotions, Transfers & Grade Changes
- Structured form workflow comparing Current vs Proposed assignment.
- Embedded policy checklist (Tenure, Appraisal Support, Disciplinary check).
- Multi-level approval sign-offs (Recommending Manager $\rightarrow$ HR $\rightarrow$ Managing Director).

### Disciplinary Action Records
- Incident logging and misconduct categorization across 11 standard categories.
- Hearing record tracking, warning levels, suspension days, and appeal rights.

### Leave Management Engine
- Annual leave accrual at 2.5 days per month (30 days per year).
- Balance calculation, request submission, and supervisor review.

### Monthly Payroll Engine
- Progressive tax calculation (South Sudan NRA PIT & Djibouti PTS).
- Overtime calculation (Standard hours, Normal 1.5x, Holiday 2.0x).
- Draft processing, finalization, and payslip generation.
